Decisions for stakeholders

Four calls are needed before any of this moves to the live site. Each is independent.

Pick a direction

  • Signal CaptureThe site reads as a live oscilloscope capture: near-black grid, bone-white dots and lines, scroll as the timebase, each initiative a channel (CH1, CH2, CH3).
  • Apple product page, played straightEach project presented like a product launch: black hardware chapters, centred type, one scrubbed animation per page, light closing chapters.
  • A mixFor example, the Apple page layout with Signal's dot-and-line language. Possible because content, the dot animation engine, icons and board drawings are already shared by both.

Confirm club facts

  • SIDEKICK: the name, "legacy" project status, and "design paused mid-way". Hardware facts from the knowledgebase: Zynq-7000 module, KiCad 9, three two-layer boards, nothing fabricated or tested.
  • SHADES: current phase ("Planning" on the mockups), the 7-phase roadmap, the two tracks, and the mentor line.
  • BRAIN: the wording "builds software with agentic tools" and "competes in hackathons". The page claims no curriculum; confirm that is right.
  • Counts: member numbers stay off the redesign until verified. The live site shows "40+ members".

Fonts: no licence exception needed

  • Round 1 used Fontshare fonts, which the design rules do not allow without an exception. Round 2 does not use them.
  • Signal Capture uses Hubot Sans, Mona Sans and Monaspace Krypton. Apple product page uses Geist and Geist Mono. SHADES uses Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading text in both. All are open-licence and self-hosted.

Fix live-site issues found in the audit

  • Blank pages: /about, /get-involved, /projects and /community render blank. 57 content blocks wait for a scroll reveal that is never switched on.
  • Project-page fonts: the smartphone and smart reading pages never load their fonts, so headings fall back to a system font (visible in the captures).
  • Red accent contrast: #d8412f fails WCAG AA as text on paper (about 4.1:1) and under white text (4.45:1).
  • Cursor: the custom crosshair cursor runs a 60 fps animation loop even when the pointer is still.
  • Deployment: the live build is from 2026-08-01; main has 26 newer commits, and digitalcpp.org does not resolve yet.
