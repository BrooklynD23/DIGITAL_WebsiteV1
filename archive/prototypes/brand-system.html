The creed

Four beliefs. Every sheet answers to them.

These are the studio's permanent terms. Copy may quote them verbatim; design may set them in the promise register; nothing may paraphrase them.

B1 · Accomplishment
Ideas aren't accomplishments. Making them real is.
Pitches, brainstorms, and mood boards don't ship. The artifact does.
B2 · Teams
The problem picks the team.
Disciplines are tools, not borders. Biology + mechanical + marketing is a normal Tuesday.
B3 · Review
Reality grades the work.
Users, tests, and the world are the final reviewers — not the org chart.
B4 · Authorship
Sign your work.
Every artifact carries names. Work nobody will own publicly isn't finished.

Names go on it

Every artifact moves through the same rail and shows where it stands. The current stage is the pencil's mark — the rail's one red element. Completed stages are ink; future stages are graphite.

One mark per viewport.

Signal red is spent like a highlighter you only get to use once — a signature, a revision tag, the current stage. If removing it loses no information, it shouldn't be red.
