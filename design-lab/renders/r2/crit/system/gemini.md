### Visual World Evaluation: Signal vs. Apple

**Scoring Note**: All ratings use a 1-10 scale, compressed toward the center (3-7) per instructions.

#### 1. Signal World
*   **Cross-Page Coherence (Score: 6/10)**: Strong unified terminal aesthetic. Nav, grid, and footers remain mostly locked. 
    *   *Inconsistency*: Grid subdivisions change density unannounced on BRAIN (01:31). 
*   **Motion Grammar (Score: 6/10)**: Mechanical, scroll-scrub driven. Verbs (forming, exploding, wiring) correctly map to specific technical actions. 
    *   *Concurrency/Loops*: Minimal ambient looping. Rarely more than one element moving, prioritizing singular focus. Timing matches scroll speed 1:1.
*   **Red Accent Rule (Score: 5/10)**: Mostly adheres to the "one trigger per viewport" rule, acting as a system cursor. 
    *   *Violation*: SHADES page (01:00) displays a red dot on the word "The" simultaneously with a red indicator on the timeline below.
*   **Project Signature Beat (Score: 6/10)**: Distinct. SIDEKICK uses vertical stacking (00:30), SHADES uses horizontal tracking (01:00), BRAIN uses orbital nodes (01:23).

#### 2. Apple World
*   **Cross-Page Coherence (Score: 5/10)**: Typography and spacing are consistent, but structural flow varies. 
    *   *Inconsistency*: The transition from dark to light mode happens at drastically different scroll depths per page (Home at 00:20; SIDEKICK at 00:30; SHADES at 01:07; BRAIN at 01:46).
*   **Motion Grammar (Score: 6/10)**: High restraint. Relies on smooth opacity fades, scaling, and spring-eased slides. 
    *   *Concurrency/Loops*: No ambient loops; entirely scroll-dependent. Elements rarely move concurrently. Easing feels universally soft and cinematic.
*   **Red Accent Rule (Score: 6/10)**: Highly restricted. Used effectively for the eye-tracking dot.
    *   *Violation*: SHADES page (01:13) places a red dot next to "Build night" text, which does not act as a CTA hover or graphic anchor.
*   **Project Signature Beat (Score: 6/10)**: Distinct. SIDEKICK focuses on 3D isometric pans (00:37), SHADES emphasizes soft focal isolation (00:53), BRAIN utilizes expansive UI window reveals (01:38).

#### 3. Head-to-Head Comparison
*   **Signal World**: 
    *   *Best*: Unmatched spatial logic; the persistent grid makes complex information feel grounded and highly technical.
    *   *Weakest*: Can feel visually exhausting; dense technical diagrams occasionally blur into the background grid (01:39).
*   **Apple World**: 
    *   *Best*: Superior typographic hierarchy and visual pacing; extreme restraint makes the hardware/software feel premium.
    *   *Weakest*: Inconsistent narrative architecture; the unpredictable dark/light section breaks disrupt the cross-site rhythm.
