### Video 1: 'Signal Capture' Critique
The scroll-scrub interaction successfully carries the state of the process, but the execution is rigid. The visual pacing feels strictly tied to linear scroll delta without easing into keyframes, making the morphing feel mechanical. While the "oscilloscope" theme implies a certain rawness, it fails the Apple-like restraint test by relying on constant ambient motion in secondary sections.

**Top 5 Worst Problems:**
1. **00:05-00:12:** Disconnect in scroll-scrub pacing: the bottom red timeline scrubs continuously, but the main left-side text snaps instantly between states without any cross-fade, creating a jarring mismatch.
2. **00:21-00:28:** Keyboard navigation on the timeline triggers instant, brutal cuts between visual states and text content with zero transition or easing.
3. **00:14-00:18:** The constant, ambient 3D rotation in the Sidekick and Brain project cards makes the viewport feel overly busy and violates the "nothing ambient" restraint principle.
4. **00:29-00:30:** Hover states on the bottom four stage cards are harsh, instantaneous border changes lacking any subtle transition easing.
5. **00:02-00:12:** The morphing animation between shapes lacks a smooth resting state; as soon as scrolling stops, the animation halts abruptly without easing into a finalized form.

### Video 2: 'Apple Product Page' Critique
This version attempts a more refined aesthetic but falls short on the details required for a true "Apple-like" feel. The scroll-scrub carries state, but the visual transitions between certain shapes feel disjointed rather than fluid. It also breaks its own rules regarding ambient motion in the hero section, and interactive elements lack the expected physics-based easing.

**Top 5 Worst Problems:**
1. **00:21-00:27:** Clicking the hero tracker dots causes the descriptive text below the shapes to snap instantly. It completely lacks the smooth cross-fade expected in this restrained design language.
2. **00:06-00:09:** The 'Prototype' and 'Test' hero states feature continuous, ambient rotation while pinned. This violates the "at most one moving thing... nothing ambient" rule; they should settle into a final position.
3. **00:07-00:08:** The scroll-scrub transition from the Test sphere to the Integrate heptagon is disjointed. Instead of vertices smoothly morphing, the sphere dissolves and the heptagon redraws, breaking the illusion of a continuous object.
4. **00:28-00:35:** The horizontal highlight carousel slides linearly and stops abruptly. It lacks the natural, spring-based easing required for a premium feel.
5. **00:03-00:09:** During the slow scroll-scrub, the text labels below the shapes fail to cross-fade smoothly. One label fades out completely before the next fades in, leaving a noticeable, unrefined empty gap.
