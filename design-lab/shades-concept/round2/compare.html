Notion findings (read 2026-10-09)

What the DIGITAL Notion SHADES pages say, set against shades.ts and DECISIONS.md. Facts: 48. Of these, 21 agree, 6 conflict, 17 are new and 4 are site claims Notion does not back. Nothing here is printed on a page yet. Every change goes through the brand agents and needs Head Designer sign-off. Raw quotes: research/notion/.

Conflicts

  1. Seats vs subteams. Notion has four subteams: Product Development, SoC/FPGA, Hardware (PCB) and Mech-E. The site's seats are Engineering, Firmware, Optics, Design and Research. Optics and Design have no Notion pages, and PCB and Mech-E are missing from the site. SHADES root, 2026-09-28
  2. One word vs words or short phrases. The first build must "render words or phrases in a fixed display region", but the site only ever says "one word at a time". Engineering Scope, 2026-07-29; ConOps, 2026-08-05
  3. Light glasses vs bulky temples. Notion keeps components off the glasses and targets "Less than 40-50 grams" with a 30/70 nose-to-ear balance. DECISIONS #7 draws heavy temples that house components. Focal Plane Adjustment, 2026-09-28; Mass Balancing, 2026-08-23
  4. "In development" vs "Planned". The root status is Planned and every task row is Not Started, but the site's meta description says "in development". SHADES root, 2026-09-28
  5. Fixed focus vs a focal slider. The first-build scope says fixed focus, which matches the site. A newer Mech-E task designs a ±4 mm focal carriage. Focal Plane Adjustment, 2026-09-28
  6. Light-path order. In Notion, controls feed a microcontroller, which feeds the FPGA. The site draws Text → Word timing → Control. Engineering Scope, 2026-07-29

New facts (printable, no parts named)

  1. Controller. It is pocket or hip sized (about 7 × 14 × 1 cm), with a speed dial and buttons for play, pause and rewind that work by touch. ConOps, 2026-08-05; Compute Puck Enclosure, 2026-09-28
  2. Cable. One cable carries both power and data between the controller and the glasses. USB-C module, 2026-09-23
  3. See-through, now sourced. The optics return the image "while preserving a view of the surrounding environment". Backs DECISIONS #6. Background & Project Solution, 2026-09-28
  4. Aim. "The central engineering challenge is therefore not to maximize reading speed. It is to control the delivery of text in a manner that supports the construction of meaning." Background, 2026-09-28
  5. Research questions. The study would evaluate "comprehension, reading speed, retention, comfort". These are questions, not outcomes. Background, 2026-09-28
  6. Settings and safety. Phrase length, punctuation pauses, font size and contrast are adjustable. If the link drops, the display goes blank. Engineering Scope; ConOps
  7. Pace. The site demo's 100–600 wpm in 25-wpm steps matches Notion's "possible MVP range", which Notion marks as not final. SoC Logic Design, 2026-09-22

Do not print: the part names (FPGA board, microcontroller, connector, haptic driver), the optics type, display resolution, the 8-month proposal, the inspiration products, or Notion's "assistive … ADHD … speed reading" line, which reads as an efficacy claim.

What each approach gains or must change

Product reveal

  • Gains: a dial and two buttons on the controller box; a cable caption ("one cable, power and data"); a Notion source for the see-through view; "Is this real?" can quote status Planned and the aim line.
  • Must change: the seat rows (conflict 1). Decide the temple bulk (conflict 3). In anatomy, put Control before Word timing (conflict 6).

Hold still

  • Gains: Notion's own mechanism line backs the slider: RSVP "reduces the need for eye scanning, line tracking, and repeated regressions" (Product Definition, 2026-09-10). The Phrase step now matches "words or phrases", and the pace beat matches the 100–600 range.
  • Must change: the join beat (conflict 1). Keep the mechanism line out of benefit wording, since brand-guardian checks it for efficacy.

Research platform

  • Gains the most: the Scope page's in and out lists match almost line for line. It can add the four research questions, the aim line, the safe blank display and "medical-device approval out of scope".
  • Must change: the seats (conflict 1), "words or short phrases" in the scope list (conflict 2) and the stage wording (conflict 4).
