## Patterns

1. **Status lives in the CTA and FAQ, not the hero.** Pages: XREAL AURA ("No. This is an early reservation"), Brilliant Halo ("ORDER now"), SPECS ("deposit ... is not an order confirmation"). [F]
2. **The word "prototype" is avoided; "glimpse" and "internal" carry the meaning.** Pages: Meta Orion (summary: "While not available to the public", "internal product"; verify verbatim). [F, summary]
3. **Hero is a product photo or a short two-part headline, not an abstract idea.** Pages: Even G1 ("Useful. Then essential."), XREAL AURA ("Your world More possibilities"), Brilliant Halo (open-source headline). Meta Orion's hero is text-first. [F]
4. **A "what is it?" definition block, then long photo sections.** Pages: XREAL AURA ("Not a headset. Not display glasses."), Meta Orion ("Behind the lens"), Even G1 (14 sections of photo plus copy). [F]
5. **Pricing and availability as the last section, dated and honest.** Pages: Apple Vision Pro ("Pricing and Availability", "early next year"), XREAL AURA (price cap, "announced closer to launch"). [F]

Not verified: Snap developer-page wording, Google Android XR official wording, HoloLens developer-edition wording, all motion/scroll behaviour on Apple and Meta, Apple's launch film. Next action: open the Snap developers page and the Google blog post in a browser and copy the status line verbatim.
