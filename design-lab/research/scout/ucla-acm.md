# ACM at UCLA — https://www.uclaacm.com
Category: student org exemplar (largest CS community at UCLA). Why: multi-committee structure shown as sub-brands (acm.studio, acm.design, acm.cyber...), wins chapter-website awards. Pick reason: how a big student org shows many disciplines under one roof.
Capture: standalone headless Chromium; refs in design-lab/references/ucla-acm/.

## Narrative
1. Nav: logo chip, committees / initiatives / events / about, outlined "member" button
2. Hero: pattern field of blue rounded-square tiles (varying tints) framing a single tagline
3. One-paragraph description: "all backgrounds, interests, skill levels... open to everyone"
4. Social icon row, then 9 committees as sub-brand lockups (own icon, colour, one-line promise, e.g. "Form follows function")
5. Sponsors, "In the News" (award headlines, ICPC World Finals results)
## Type
Poppins (H1 36.8/700, H2 24/700), Open Sans 16/400 body; Lexend/Inter also loaded. Ink rgb(51,51,51).
## Color
White ground, one electric blue (~#2a6bff) for hero tile pattern; each committee has its own accent (pink, orange, green, teal).
## Layout: centered 760px intro, 3-col committee grid, short page (~3000px). Next.js site.
## Motion: not observed (static capture).
## Project/people/CTA
- Structure over projects: committees are the product; proof = news + award headlines (competition placements), not member showcases.
- Join: outlined "member" button in nav + login; events open to everyone regardless of major.
- Sponsors in their own section.
## Mobile: captured in mobile.png (not analysed in detail).
## Steal as principle
1. Named sub-teams as lockups with a one-line promise each (maps to disciplines).
2. Tile/pixel pattern as a brandable hero field.
3. "Open regardless of major or experience" stated once, plainly.
## Avoid
1. Rounded-everything cartoon icons. 2. Multicolour per-committee accents without a rule. 3. Little project evidence on homepage.
