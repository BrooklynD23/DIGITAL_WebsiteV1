- People appear as recognition ("named fellows") and tiny monochrome headshot rows, not bios.

## Steal as principle
1. A small custom glyph/badge per team or project, reused everywhere as identity.
2. Metadata footer on every project tile: source, date, group, tags.
3. Square-tile grid that mixes content types without cards-with-shadows.
## Avoid
1. Dense news-wall is wrong for a student org with few projects. 2. Vague "imagine" taglines. 3. Left nav hidden behind hero on first view.
