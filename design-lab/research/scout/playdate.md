# Playdate — https://play.date
Category: consumer product (hardware + games). Shots: design-lab/references/playdate/
Why good: a tiny odd product sold with warmth and one yellow; humour, real 3D model, and a story told as numbered short sections ("The System. The Design. The Crank. The Season.").

## Narrative
1. Sticker-style season badge + simple nav (Games, Dev, Education, Help, Sign In, purple Shop pill).
2. Hero: interactive 3D device (Three.js) on grey, "Hello." on screen, plain-language one-liner ("a new, tiny handheld game system ... made just for fun"), huge yellow wordmark, purple CTA.
3. Switch to full-width yellow (#ffc833) sections, one idea each, short H2 with a full stop.
4. Games catalog, "Your games?" (make your own: free SDK, Pulp browser game maker - audience for builders), "Big screen optional.", Education band (teaching programming), price block "All for just $229", specs table.
5. Season/preorder CTA repeated.

## Type
Roobert 700-800 (rounded geometric grotesk). H2 ~68px/68, body ~20px; headings are full sentences, bold, tight.

## Color
Tokens: --brand-yellow #ffc833, --darkest-gray #1d1c18, --screen-black #312f28, --screen-white #b1afa8, --mid-gray #7e7b75; CTA purple. Grey hero -> yellow sections.

## Layout
Centred ~800px reading column in big colour fields; wide product mockups break out. Section = one claim + one image.

## Motion
Three.js global + canvas present (device render appears to be 3D; interaction not tested); 2 videos. Static capture only.

## Product / proof / CTA
Product shown as hero 3D object, screens of games and tools; proof = catalog and education use; CTAs: a purple pill (Shop) repeated, plus a quieter "Playdate Developer Site" for makers (audience split like Rive).

## Mobile
No overflow. Logo wraps to two lines, hamburger + Shop pill; device, sentence, giant wordmark, CTA stacked; yellow starts directly below.

**Steal:** (1) a short declarative sentence + full stop as section titles; (2) a dedicated "make your own" band for builders; (3) one saturated field colour carrying whole sections.
**Avoid:** (1) cute branding too far from an engineering org; (2) saturated purple/yellow pairing we do not own; (3) giant wordmark repeats.
Tech: Three.js, 2 videos, Roobert; no GSAP/Lenis detected.
