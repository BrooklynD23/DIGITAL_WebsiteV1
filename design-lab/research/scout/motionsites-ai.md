# MotionSites AI — SCOUT-1

URL: https://motionsites.ai (verified exists; shots: design-lab/references/motionsites-ai/). Category: marketplace of AI website PROMPTS (500+ animated landing/hero prompts, looping video backgrounds, MCP, "Animated Backgrounds", Academy). Pricing per third-party listings: 3 free prompts, then $279/yr or $399 lifetime (not verified on site). It does not host/generate sites: you paste prompts into Lovable/Bolt/Cursor/Claude.

## Site (desktop.png)
- Dark #121212, 56px/900 all-caps hero "UNLOCK YOUR AI DESIGN SUPERPOWERS" with glow text, pill filters (All, Hero, Landing, SaaS, Agency, Creative, Portfolio, 3D...), masonry grid of video-preview cards with crown icon for premium. System font stack. 1 reduced-motion rule. Several cards were still loading (grey placeholders).
- Content is its own product demo: looping video hero backgrounds, huge bold type over video, glass/blur overlays.

## Relevance to DIGITAL
Mostly an ANTI-reference per brief §39: its templates trend glow, glass, gradients, pills, generic AI-hero. Useful only as a map of what to avoid and for the "looping video behind big type" pattern (needs poster + reduced-motion fallback + lazy-load; never autoplay on mobile data-saver).
I did not open paid prompts; no prompt text was copied.

## Steal as principle
1. Preview-video cards with category filter = quick-scan gallery (apply to project index, real footage only).
2. Big bold type over motion media can work when contrast is controlled (scrim).
3. Copy-to-clipboard affordance per item (could suit specs/code snippets in docs).

## Avoid
1. Glow text, pill overload, glass panels, generic gradient heroes (all in brief §39).
2. Stock/AI-generated loop videos presented as product proof.
3. Prompt-pasting as design process: output looks like every other template.
