# Entrepreneurs First — https://www.joinef.com
Category: accelerator / talent investor. Why: bold, near-brutalist typographic landing: electric violet ground, orange serif caps, bracketed nav labels. Pick reason: strongest example of a recruiting-first page with immediate "you might have nothing" inclusive framing.
Capture: standalone headless Chromium; refs in design-lab/references/entrepreneurs-first/. Cookie banner overlays hero in the screenshot.

## Narrative
1. Full-viewport violet hero: stacked serif caps with arrow glyph; two paragraphs addressed to "you" (idea / cofounder / nothing)
2. Pale-teal band: single large testimonial with founder name + backer
3. Numbered claims as H3 sentences in caps: cofounder matching, idea-to-revenue, investor backing, terms, portfolio
4. "Interested?" apply block + newsletter + hiring links
## Type
PS Times (H1 74px/400, orange rgb(255,116,72)) paired with neue-haas-grotesk-display (18px/700 body). Section claims in uppercase.
## Color
Violet ~#6600e0 bg, orange #ff7448 type, pale teal rgb(223,234,234) bands, near-black text. Dashed hairline rules. High-contrast, three-colour system.
## Layout: left-aligned stagger (indented lines), 1 column ~650px copy, [START] / [MENU] bracketed labels at the corners. Page ~6350px. WordPress-built (--wp-- vars).
## Motion: not observed beyond scroll. Static strength.
## Project/people/CTA
- Founders shown as names + exit/backer line, linked outward; metrics ($ valuation) as authority.
- Eligibility lowered ("you might have nothing") then a single apply route. Three distinct paths for three audiences: apply / stay in the loop / work here.
## Mobile: captured in mobile.png (not analysed in detail).
## Steal as principle
1. A limited 3-colour system with a saturated ground makes a page unmistakable.
2. Bracketed mono-feel labels ([START]) as a navigation voice.
3. Address the "I have no idea yet" visitor explicitly in the recruiting copy.
## Avoid
1. Purple/violet ground drifts toward crypto/AI palette flagged in BRIEF §39. 2. Billion-dollar proof. 3. Cookie banner covering hero.
## Tech observed: WordPress; no gsap/THREE/Lenis globals.
