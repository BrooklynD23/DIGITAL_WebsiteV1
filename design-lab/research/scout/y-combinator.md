# Y Combinator — https://www.ycombinator.com
Category: accelerator. Why: typographic restraint (serif display, italic accent) and a proof section built from tiny faces inline in text. Pick reason: model for evidence-led credibility and a single apply CTA.
Capture: standalone headless Chromium; refs in design-lab/references/y-combinator/.

## Narrative
1. Hero: large serif claim with italic key phrase + footnote-style quote below; chevron to scroll; nav w/ black Apply pill
2. "In founders' words": paragraph of testimonials as running text, each sentence ending in a 32px circular headshot (attribution without cards)
3. "Be in the room with..." partner/people photo trio
4. "All partners were YC founders first" partner profiles
5. Before/after company cases with valuations, then Knowledge & News, CTA
## Type
Source Serif 4 (display + body), Outfit (UI, 14px/300, uppercase label 11px/1.68px tracking), DM Sans. Heading is large serif (size not extracted), italic on the key phrase. Ink rgb(22,20,15).
## Color
Warm paper rgb(245,245,238), near-black ink, one orange (#ff6600-ish logo tile), black pill CTA. Almost no other colour.
## Layout: centered 520px reading column, large vertical rhythm, 3-up photo row. Page ~11,700px.
## Motion: minimal; fade/scroll cues. No hijack.
## Project/people/CTA
- Proof = numbers (portfolio value) + before/after photos of founders; testimonials de-carded into prose with avatar chips.
- Apply CTA: pill in nav + closing section, copy lowers the bar ("never too early").
- Partners presented as peers: "founders first".
## Mobile: captured in mobile.png (not analysed in detail).
## Steal as principle
1. Testimonials as flowing prose with avatar chips instead of testimonial cards (answers brief's "no random testimonials" - but only with real quotes).
2. Before/after pairing as proof of change.
3. Serif + italic emphasis on one phrase, with a footnote-style definition of the key word.
## Avoid
1. Valuation/dollar boasting (wrong for a student org). 2. Elite-gatekeeping tone ("top 1%"). 3. Quote from a famous person as hero crutch.
## Tech observed: no gsap/THREE/Lenis globals; slick carousel font loaded.
