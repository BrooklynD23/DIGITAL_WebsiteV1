DIGITAL · badge prototype · 2026-11-12

A chrome member badge that catches the light

The badge invites a visitor to picture their own name on a team. Here it is a brushed-chrome card with a holographic film: move the pointer over it and the rainbow slides with the light, the card tilts toward you. Click or press Enter and one bright band sweeps across, the film flares, and the next open role appears.

Hover to tilt. Click or press Enter to flash and show the next role. On a phone, tap.

How it works

  • Chrome base: layered grey gradients with fine brushed lines. No image files.
  • Holographic film: a rainbow band in color-dodge blend whose position follows the pointer, so the colours slide as the light moves. At rest it stays faint (35%).
  • Tilt: up to 8° toward the pointer, easing back over 420ms when the pointer leaves.
  • Click: a white band sweeps across in 900ms, the film flares to full and shifts hue, then settles. The role advances on each click, so the visitor can browse the open seats.
  • Reduced motion: no tilt, no sweep, the film holds still. Click still advances the role.
  • Accessibility: focusable, Enter and Space work, the label lists every open role.

What this needs from you

  1. Colour exception: the rainbow film is a full-spectrum effect in a system with one red and greys. It would be the second named exception after the BRAIN orb.
  2. Shadow and depth: the current badge is flat by rule (no shadow, no rotation, DESIGN §11 and §18). Chrome reads as metal only with highlights and a tilt, so this needs that rule lifted for the badge.
  3. Where: home join and SHADES join, as now.
