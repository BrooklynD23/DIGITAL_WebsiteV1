Product definition: VESSEL

A virtual factory that turns recycled aluminum into a finished smartphone enclosure, simulated from raw material to warehouse.

Sep 30, 2026 · TWINS · Product definition

What VESSEL is

VESSEL (Virtual Ecosystem for System Simulation & Enterprise Logistics) is the first TWINS project. Students research, design and simulate an end-to-end manufacturing and supply-chain system with discrete-event simulation or digital-twin software.

Material recoveryProcessingAnnealingFabricationFinishingInspectionPackagingWarehouse

The engineering goal

Turn each stage of that value stream into a working simulation. Students define operating parameters such as equipment capacity, cycle time, setup time, labor, energy use, material flow, defect rates, maintenance, inspection, inventory policy, packaging and transport limits. The model produces synthetic production data like a real factory's.

Why it is different

Most projects study manufacturing, quality, operations and logistics separately. VESSEL asks how a decision in one stage changes the rest: teams compare production philosophies, find bottlenecks, investigate defects, balance capacity, cut waste and redesign workflows, then measure cost, throughput, utilization, inventory, lead time, sustainability, quality and delivery.
